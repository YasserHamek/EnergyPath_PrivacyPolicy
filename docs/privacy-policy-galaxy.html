Energy Path Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Summary: Energy Path respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your game progress is stored locally on your device only.

Information We Do Not Collect

Energy Path does not collect any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers for tracking purposes
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Any data for advertising purposes

Local Data Storage

Energy Path stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Game Progress: Level completion status, star ratings, and current level
  • Achievements: Unlocked achievements and progress toward goals
  • Statistics: Play time, levels completed, and performance metrics
  • Settings: Sound, haptics, accessibility, and language preferences
  • Purchase Status: Record of in-app purchases made

This data is stored using Android SharedPreferences and never leaves your device. There is no cloud sync feature in this version.

In-App Purchases

Energy Path offers optional in-app purchases processed entirely through Samsung Galaxy Store. We do not receive or store any payment information. Purchase records are managed through Samsung In-App Purchase (IAP) and stored locally to unlock content.

Third-Party Services

Energy Path does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only external service used is Samsung In-App Purchase (IAP) for in-app purchases.

Children's Privacy

Energy Path is suitable for all ages and does not collect any personal information from children or adults.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data
  • You can reset progress within the app via Settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
